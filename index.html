ElderBuddy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 February 2026

ElderBuddy (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the ElderBuddy mobile application (the “Service”).

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: If you create an account, we may collect your name, email address, or unique user ID.
  • Voice Input: If microphone features are enabled, Voice input is processed to generate text transcriptions and responses. Raw audio recordings are not stored.
  • Conversation Transcripts: Text conversations and generated transcripts may be stored to provide personalization and memory features.
  • Usage & Diagnostics: Limited technical data such as crash logs or performance metrics may be collected to improve stability.

How We Use Information

  • To operate and maintain the Service.
  • To personalize conversations and memory features.
  • To improve performance, reliability, and security.
  • To respond to support inquiries.

Data Storage & Retention

Conversation data and user preferences may be stored on secure cloud servers. We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the Service and for legitimate operational purposes. Users may request deletion of their data at any time. We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect user data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may use trusted third-party service providers (such as cloud hosting or AI processing providers) to operate the Service. These providers are contractually required to protect your information.

Permissions

The app may request access to the microphone for voice features. You can disable permissions at any time in your device settings.

Children’s Privacy

ElderBuddy is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting us at the email below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.
